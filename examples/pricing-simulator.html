How the numbers are computed
  • Signups today = customers × (growth + churn). With 400, 8% and 4% that is 48 a month.
  • Each month at price P: signups = signups% × [ (1 − share) × 48 + share × 12% × current customers ]. The share slider blends a flat pipeline with compounding growth.
  • New-price customers: N(t) = N(t−1) × (1 − churn_P) + signups. Existing customers: L(t) = L(t−1) × (1 − churn) at today's price if kept; if moved, they pay P from month 1, churn at churn_P, and the one-off churn is taken in month 1.
  • MRR(t) = L(t) × price_L + N(t) × P. ARR run-rate is MRR × 12. Revenue over the horizon sums MRR from month 1 to the last month.
  • Lifetime value = P ÷ monthly churn, revenue only, before gross margin and acquisition cost.
  • Break-even is the lowest signups% at which price P's MRR at the last month equals staying at today's price with today's signups and churn.
  • Presets set signups% = (P ÷ today's price)^−e and churn = today's churn × (P ÷ today's price)^c. Low: e 0.3, c 0.1. Medium: e 0.6, c 0.25. High: e 1.0, c 0.4. Constant elasticity is generous to big jumps; real demand curves usually have a cliff somewhere.
